### Voice and Stem Assignment

- **Measures with cymbals/hi-hat** (Pre-Choruses, Choruses, Outro): **Two independent voices** — **Voice 1 (stems UP)** holds cymbals & hi-hat (Crash `49`, Closed Hi-Hat `42`, Open Hi-Hat `46`), and **Voice 2 (stems DOWN)** holds Kick (`36`) and Snare (`38`). Because Kick and Snare never coincide on the same 16th-note slot in any of Patterns `[1]`–`[5]`, Voice 2 beams the entire linear kick-snare groove cleanly while simultaneous cymbal/hi-hat hits sit in Voice 1 (`484` simultaneous-limb events verified in MIDI).
- **Measures with Kick + Snare only** (Intro, Verses, Bridge, Breakdown — Pattern `[1]`): Notated cleanly in **Voice 1 with stems DOWN** so no redundant floating whole rests clutter the top of the staff while preserving the consistent stems-down kick/snare visual convention throughout.

### Articulations and Special Techniques

- **Accents (`>`):** Engraved as native MuseScore `<Articulation>` accents on Snare hits (beats `2`, `3&`, `4&`), section-opening Crash Cymbal hits, and the final beat-3 cutoff (`m. 114` / played `m. 174`, with staccato).
- **Open vs Closed Hi-Hat:** Engraved as two **separate drumset instruments** (`Closed Hi-Hat` MIDI `42` with `cross` notehead vs `Open Hi-Hat` MIDI `46` with `xcircle` notehead), verified to trigger distinct audio samples in `Muse Percussion / Drum Kit`.
- **Ghost notes, flams, drags, rolls, hi-hat pedal:** None occur in either `tTabs #257044` or the studio recording's tight syncopated groove; no MuseScore substitutions were required.

## 5. Entry, Independent Audio Verification and Reconciliations

The score was generated programmatically into MusicXML (with MusicXML's 1-indexed `<midi-unpitched>` = GM + 1 verified after import so no off-by-one pitch shift occurred), imported into MuseScore Studio 4.7.7, patched to `instrumentId="drumset"` with the complete 10-voice `<Drum>` definition and explicit `audiosettings.json`, and audited bar-by-bar against both the **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 US studio recording** (`Nine_Inch_Nails_-_03_-_Letting_You.mp3`) and `tTabs #257044`:

- **Acoustic confirmation of Patterns `[1]`–`[5]`:**
  - **Pattern `[1]` (mm. 1–28, 53–72, 97–104, 129–151):** 16-slot low-band (`<150 Hz`) peaks landed squarely on slots `0, 6, 9, 12` (`34.9, 35.0, 32.2, 35.7`) = beats `1, 2&, 3e, 4`, and mid-band snare peaks landed on slots `4, 10, 14` (`25.3, 24.6, 24.2`) = beats `2, 3&, 4&`, matching `tTabs #257044`'s core groove exactly.
  - **Pattern `[2]` (Pre-Choruses, played mm. 29–36, 73–80, 105–112):** High-frequency (`>4 kHz`) energy jumps `+3.5` to `+4.5 dB` across 8th-note slots (`0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14`), confirming 8th-note Closed Hi-Hat.
  - **Pattern `[3]` (Choruses, played mm. 37–52, 81–96, 113–128):** High-frequency energy jumps another `+3.6` to `+5.6 dB` into a dense wash matching `|xxxx|-xxx|xx-x|xx-x|`.
  - **Patterns `[4]` & `[5]` (Outro, played mm. 152–173):** Low-band 16-slot measurement directly confirmed double-kick 16ths on beat 4 (`slots 12, 13 = 40, 47`) at played `m. 152` and four consecutive 16th kicks on beat 1 (`slots 0..3 = 36, 47, 41, 49`) at played `m. 153`.
- **Documented divergences & refinements from `tTabs #257044` (by bar number):**
  1. **Tempo (`182 BPM` vs `180 BPM`):** Adjusted from `The Kiw`'s approximate `180 BPM` to the acoustically measured `182 BPM` (`229.45 s` vs `229.44 s` studio audio span).
  2. **Crash Cymbal accents (engraved mm. 17, 25, 45, 53, 69, 77, 100, 101, 109, 113, 114 → played mm. 29, 37, 45, 73, 81, 89, 105, 113, 121, 152, 153, 161, 169, 173, 174):** Added where beat-1 `>5 kHz` downbeat transients spiked sharply (`42.3–57.0`) in the licensed recording; `tTabs #257044` omits cymbals entirely.
  3. **Open Hi-Hat in Choruses (engraved mm. 25–32, 53–60, 77–84 → played mm. 37–52, 81–96, 113–128):** Notated as `Open Hi-Hat` (`46`, `xcircle`) rather than closed hi-hat based on the sustained high-frequency wash (`HF_avg = 45.5–49.0`) in the studio audio.
  4. **Final measure cutoff (engraved `m. 114` / played `m. 174`):** `tTabs #257044` ends with `[5 & 4] 11x each` without an ending cutoff. Slot-by-slot audio analysis of played `m. 174` (`228.18 s`) showed strong hits through slot `8` (beat 3: `K=49`, `HF=27`) followed by complete silence across slots `9–15` (`S=0`, `HF=0`), so `m. 114` is notated as beat 1 four 16th kicks + Crash, beat 2 Snare/Kick + HH, beat 3 accented staccato Crash+Kick cutoff, and beat 4 rest.
  5. **Unspecified fills:** Neither the studio track nor `tTabs #257044` contains tom fills; none were invented.

## 8. Known Limitations

1. **No live human listening test:** Audio playback was verified through automated 7-voice synthesis tests, MIDI repeat/pitch/limb audits, floating-point `astats` headroom/RMS checks, preset-swap proof and deterministic reopen MD5s, not by listening through speakers/headphones.
2. **Rendered PDF preview instead of live GUI screenshot:** As documented in the sibling experiment on this GNOME Wayland desktop (`DISPLAY=:0`), X11 window capture (`import -window`) is blocked by Wayland compositing (`Resource temporarily unavailable`); [`screenshots/score-page-1.png`](screenshots/score-page-1.png) is therefore a page-1 preview rendered from `nine-inch-nails-letting-you.pdf` showing the Drumset staff, noteheads, voices, repeat signs and section markings.
3. **Ride cymbal and toms unused in song:** While Ride Cymbal 1 (`51`), Pedal Hi-Hat (`44`) and Toms (`48`/`45`/`41`) are defined in the score's Drumset map (and Ride/Pedal HH verified in the 7-voice audio check), the studio track's groove uses Kick, Snare, Closed Hi-Hat, Open Hi-Hat and Crash Cymbal only.
